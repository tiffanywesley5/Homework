About this site

This site is a quick guide for aspiring beauty founders who want to turn their handmade products into a real business. Whether you're selling at local markets or building an online brand, these basics will help you get started with confidence.

Top tips for beauty founders

  • Always calculate your true cost of goods — including your own labor
  • Test your products with real customers before scaling
  • Learn about MoCRA compliance early
  • Don't underprice your products just to compete
